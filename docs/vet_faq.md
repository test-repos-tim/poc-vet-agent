# Paws & Claws Animal Hospital — FAQ for POC Testing

## Emergency
**Q: Do you handle emergencies?**
A: Yes. During business hours, call (555) 123-4567 or bring your pet in. For life-threatening emergencies after hours, contact the nearest 24-hour veterinary hospital.

**Q: What is a pet emergency?**
A: Difficulty breathing, severe bleeding, trauma, seizures, poisoning, collapse, or inability to urinate. If in doubt, call us.

## Appointments
**Q: How do I book an appointment?**
A: Call (555) 123-4567 or use the chat widget. Provide pet name, species, preferred date/time, and reason.

**Q: What information do I need to book?**
A: Pet name, species (dog/cat/other), preferred date and time, and reason for visit (vaccine, checkup, illness, etc.).

**Q: Can I cancel or reschedule?**
A: Yes. Call at least 24 hours before your appointment.

## Prescriptions
**Q: Can I refill a prescription?**
A: Yes. Provide pet name, medication name, dosage, and pharmacy contact.

**Q: How long do refills take?**
A: Usually 24-48 hours. We will contact you when ready.

## Hours & Location
**Q: What are your hours?**
A: Mon-Fri 8am-8pm, Sat 9am-5pm, Sun Closed.

**Q: Where are you located?**
A: 123 Main Street, Springfield.

**Q: Is there parking?**
A: Yes, free parking in front of the clinic.

## Services
**Q: What services do you offer?**
A: Emergency care, wellness exams, vaccinations, surgery, dental care, prescriptions.

**Q: Do you treat exotic pets?**
A: Dogs and cats primarily. Call to ask about other species.
